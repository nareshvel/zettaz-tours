Departure Assignment Roles vs Staff Roles

Departure assignment roles (e.g. guide, driver, skipper) are job labels on departure_assignments.assignment_role. They are distinct from the system Staff Role.

Example: A person with the Staff role Driver who is assigned as skipper for a departure will still only see that assigned trip on their "Today" screen.
